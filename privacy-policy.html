Privacy Policy – NZN STUDY HUB Last updated: 31 October 2026 Contact: nadeemzarifnizarproduction@gmail.com 1. Introduction NZN STUDY HUB is an educational application designed to help students practise questions and improve their learning. We respect users' privacy and aim to collect as little personal information as possible. 2. Information and Data Storage NZN STUDY HUB V50 does not require users to create an account or log in. The app does not contain an advertising SDK or analytics SDK in the source code reviewed. The app stores the following learning information locally on the user's device: - Quiz scores and correct answers - Coins and badges - Selected class or course - Quiz progress and question history This information is stored using the device's local browser storage. It is not sent to an app-operated account or database by the reviewed app code. 3. Advertising and Analytics The reviewed V50 source code does not include advertisements or an analytics integration. 4. Third-Party Services The app is hosted using GitHub Pages and may be distributed through Google Play. These platforms may process certain technical information under their own privacy policies and practices. 5. Data Sharing We do not intentionally sell or share locally stored quiz progress with third parties. 6. Data Deletion Users can generally delete locally stored app information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. This may permanently remove saved progress. 7. Children's Privacy NZN STUDY HUB is an educational application intended for students. The reviewed V50 app does not ask users to submit personal information through a login or registration system. 8. Security We take reasonable steps to maintain the app and protect users' information. However, no electronic storage system can guarantee complete security. 9. Policy Updates This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy webpage. 10.
